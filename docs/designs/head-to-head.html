Scouting log Burgess ⇄ Halverson
Last edited by P. Delaney · 2026-04-03 · 6 revisions

Serve & return

  • Serves big and flat on the ad side; second serve is a kicker to the BH. Step in on the 2nd.
  • Left-handed — his deuce-side slice serve pulls righties off the court. Stand a step toward the doubles alley when returning.
  • Against Burgess singles: loses tempo when we hit deep returns down the middle. See 2025-05-04 QF — split-step return strategy worked after set 1.

Patterns — doubles

  • Pairs with R. Huberman 90% of the time. They run the I-formation on big points — poacher breaks before the serve lands. Lob early returns.
  • Struggles with high balls to BH when at net. M. Baird scored 3 easy points off this in 2024-09-14 SF. Worth seeding into every return game.
  • Crosscourt dink volleys from the ad side are his weakest — he floats them when moving forward.

Stamina / late-match

  • Super-TB split: 1–3 against us. He fades after 90 minutes; his first-serve % drops noticeably. If we can push to a third, odds tilt our way.
  • Twice he has called a medical/time-out in a third set (2025-10-18, 2024-03-23). Don't let him reset the rhythm — stay close to the chair between points.

Captain reminders

  • He appealed mid-2025 to stay at 4.5A; won the appeal. Expect aggressive line-calling — he knows his rating is being watched.
